03

Who should stay with Gorgias

Stay with Gorgias if you run support. A team working tickets, email, chat, voice and SMS in one place, an AI that resolves support cases and is billed per resolution: ARLing Asistent replaces none of that. It has no inbox, creates no tickets and never hands a conversation to a person.

Stay as well if your questions are mostly about existing orders: where is my parcel, can I return this, was I charged twice. ARLing Asistent knows your products and contact details, not your orders.

Consider ARLing Asistent if the questions are about the products themselves, you have no support team to feed a helpdesk, or you want a widget that keeps none of your shoppers' conversations. It reads your public products.json feed and needs one script tag.

04

FAQ

Is ARLing Asistent a helpdesk?

No. No inbox, no tickets, no agent seats. It answers product questions from your feed and, when it cannot, tells the shopper to write to your contact email. No ticket is created.

How does it get onto my Shopify store today?

Fill in the trial form on the main page with https://your-store.myshopify.com/products.json and your email, get a tenant ID, and add one script tag before </body> in theme.liquid. A one-click Shopify app is in preparation.

Does it know my orders or customers?

No. It knows the product feed (title, description, price, link, image) and the contact details you enter. It has no access to orders, accounts or carts.
